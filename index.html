ANTIPLATELET DRUGS

Classification of Antiplatelet Drugs

  • Cyclooxygenase Inhibitors: Aspirin
  • ADP Receptor Antagonists: Clopidogrel, Prasugrel, Ticagrelor, Ticlopidine
  • Glycoprotein IIb/IIIa Inhibitors: Abciximab, Tirofiban, Eptifibatide
  • Phosphodiesterase Inhibitors: Dipyridamole, Cilostazol
  • Protease-Activated Receptor-1 Antagonist: Vorapaxar

ANTIPLATELET ACTION OF ASPIRIN

Mechanism of Action

Aspirin irreversibly inhibits cyclooxygenase (COX-1) enzyme by acetylating serine residue at position 530.

This prevents the conversion of arachidonic acid to thromboxane A2 (TXA2) in platelets.

Result: Decreased platelet aggregation and reduced thrombus formation. The effect lasts for the lifespan of the platelet (7-10 days).

THERAPEUTIC USES

Indications for Antiplatelet Drugs

  • Acute coronary syndrome (unstable angina, myocardial infarction)
  • Prevention of stroke and transient ischemic attacks (TIA)
  • Following coronary artery bypass graft (CABG) surgery
  • Following percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) with stent placement
  • Peripheral arterial disease
  • Atrial fibrillation (as alternative or adjunct to anticoagulation)
  • Prevention of cardiovascular events in high-risk patients

FIBRINOLYTICS

List of Fibrinolytic Agents

  • First Generation: Streptokinase, Urokinase
  • Second Generation: Alteplase (rt-PA), Anistreplase (APSAC)
  • Third Generation: Reteplase, Tenecteplase

Therapeutic Uses

  • Acute ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI)
  • Acute ischemic stroke (within therapeutic window)
  • Massive pulmonary embolism
  • Deep vein thrombosis (selected cases)
  • Peripheral arterial occlusion

ANTIFIBRINOLYTICS

List of Antifibrinolytic Agents

  • Lysine Analogues: Tranexamic acid, Epsilon-aminocaproic acid (EACA)
  • Serine Protease Inhibitor: Aprotinin

Therapeutic Uses

  • Control of bleeding during cardiac surgery
  • Management of menorrhagia (heavy menstrual bleeding)
  • Prevention of bleeding in hemophilia patients undergoing dental procedures
  • Treatment of hereditary angioedema
  • Control of bleeding in trauma and postpartum hemorrhage
  • Prevention of rebleeding in subarachnoid hemorrhage
