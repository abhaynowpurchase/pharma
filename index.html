⚠️ Clinical Pearl

Steroid-induced IOP rise typically occurs 2–6 weeks after initiation. Monitor IOP closely in high-risk patients (glaucoma suspects, high myopes, young children). Soft steroids (loteprednol) carry the lowest IOP risk.
