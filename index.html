10-Day Sprint Structure

Wednesday to Next Friday Cycle

Sprint Start: Wednesday Morning • Sprint End: Next Friday Evening • Duration: 10 Business Days

Wed (Day 1)

Sprint Planning & Kickoff

Review capacity, commit to sprint scope based on story points, scope freeze after kickoff, assign tasks, clarify requirements

Thu-Fri

Early Development Week 1

Start feature implementation, create PRs early, daily standups to track progress and blockers

Mon-Wed

Active Development Week 2

Continue implementation, parallel QA testing begins, code reviews, address feedback promptly

Thu

Integration & Testing

Merge approved PRs, integration testing, fix bugs immediately, ensure all features are stable

Fri (Day 10)

Stabilization & Deployment

Final testing, deploy to production, sprint demo, retrospective, groom tickets for next sprint

⚡ 10 days provides buffer for complex features while maintaining fast feedback loops
